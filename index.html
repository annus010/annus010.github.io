  1. Research architecture研究架构图
    HYDROLOGICAL MODELINGDec. 2025 – Present2025.12 — 至今

    Physics-guided hydrological digital twin for the North China Plain面向华北平原的物理约束水文数字孪生

    Zhounan Yu

    Developing a hydrological digital twin that combines PyTorch-based neural parameterization with coupled surface-water and groundwater processes. Align heterogeneous forcing, irrigation, and observation data to a 1 km grid; calibrate model parameters against well observations; and examine water-balance consistency and long-term state evolution. Use fixed-parameter scenario experiments to diagnose how water use changes model behavior, with scientific agents supporting simulation and multi-objective decision workflows.将基于 PyTorch 的神经网络参数化与地表水—地下水耦合过程相结合，构建水文数字孪生模型。统一气象驱动、灌溉与观测数据的时空尺度，形成 1 km 网格输入；利用地下水井观测进行参数校准，检验水量平衡与长期模拟的稳定性。在固定模型参数的条件下开展情景实验，分析用水变化的影响，并探索由科学智能体支持的模拟与多目标决策。

    Methods: Python / PyTorch · geospatial alignment · differentiable parameter calibration · RMSE, MAE & bias diagnostics · water-balance checks · reproducible checkpoints.主要方法：Python / PyTorch、时空数据对齐、可微分参数校准、RMSE / MAE 与偏差分析、水量平衡检验、模型检查点保存与复现。

    Zhounan Yu et al.
    ICLR 2027Under review审稿中

  • Social–ecological resilience and adaptation社会—生态系统韧性与适应

    Wenjun Jiao, Zhounan Yu, Siyuan He

    Developed a social–ecological systems framework to study policy-driven adaptation in the Qingtian rice–fish system. Used ecosystem-service trade-offs and carbon footprint per unit output value to assess resilience. This work informs my understanding of environmental pressures, adaptation, and the consequences of model-based decisions.以青田稻鱼共生系统为例，构建社会—生态系统分析框架，研究政策如何推动农业文化遗产适应现代化。通过生态系统服务之间的权衡与单位产值碳足迹，分析系统韧性的变化。
